These are school trademarks used only to show partner schools; no edits were made to
the artwork. Ask each school before publishing if the partnership is not already
contractual.


## Processing (2026-10-21)

`etss.jpg`, `bac.jpg`, `bhs.png` and `clw.png` had white backgrounds; they were re-saved as PNGs with the white keyed out (pixels within 22 levels of neutral and above 228 made transparent, soft edge) through a canvas in headless Chrome (`tools/keywhite.mjs`). Nothing else was altered.
