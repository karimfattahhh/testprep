# ELMY school logos

Added to the "Trusted by" strip on 2026-10-28 from Karim's ELMY school-logos export (`ELMY-School-Logos.zip`,
78 school records: 30 partners, 48 relationships, collected 28 October 2026). Per the export's notes, original website files
were used where obtainable; the others are web-resolution copies found through Google. Each file here was processed by
`tools/logo-prep.mjs` (white or light background keyed out, margins trimmed) and re-encoded as a 120 px tall WebP.

Not used: the eleven schools already on the strip with official files (see `../CREDITS.md`), and repeats of a network
emblem (Al-Mustafa Ksarnaba, Sour and Hara; SABIS Adma and Choueifat; CPF Quraytem). The Al-Mustafa emblem is the
Nabatieh file, SABIS the Koura wordmark, CPF the Montana file.
